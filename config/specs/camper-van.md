# Camper Van Search Requirements

I am looking for a camper van in the UK that I can keep for at least five years
and ideally ten years.

It will initially be used by two adults and a dog. We may have a child within the
next two years, so it needs to remain practical and safe for a small family.

## Budget

- Ideal price: £6,000–£8,500
- Maximum: £10,000
- Preferably leave £1,000–£2,000 available for servicing, repairs and small
  improvements

## Essential requirements

- Preferably 2016 or newer
- Diesel must be confirmed Euro 6 and ULEZ compliant
- Working cab air-conditioning
- Maximum vehicle weight of 3,500kg or less
- At least four legal travelling seats
- Safe rear seat suitable for a future child seat
- Proper seat belts and secure seat mountings
- Sleeps at least two adults, with space for a future child
- Good service and MOT history
- No serious rust, structural corrosion, damp or roof leaks
- No major engine, gearbox, DPF, AdBlue or electrical faults
- Seller must allow an independent inspection

## Preferred type of van

Medium-sized vans are preferred, particularly:

- Peugeot Expert
- Citroën Dispatch
- Vauxhall Vivaro
- Renault Trafic
- Toyota Proace
- Ford Transit Custom

A long-wheelbase version is preferred.

A professionally installed pop-top with a proper roof bed would be ideal, as it
provides more living and sleeping space without making the van too large to
drive or park.

Small vans such as the Berlingo, Partner, Kangoo or Caddy may be too cramped for
two adults, a dog and a child.

Large vans such as the Ducato, Boxer, Relay, Master or full-size Transit may be
considered, but only if they are Euro 6, below 3,500kg and in very good
condition.

## Useful camper features

Strong preferences include:

- Rock-and-roll bed
- Pop-top roof bed
- Leisure battery
- USB charging and 12V lighting
- Good ventilation
- Diesel heater
- Fridge or powered cool box
- Basic cooking and water facilities
- Blackout curtains
- Practical storage
- Space for a dog and future child equipment

These features are less important than mechanical condition, safe seats and
emissions compliance.

## Before recommending a viewing

Try to obtain:

- Registration number
- ULEZ compliance result
- Exact engine and Euro standard
- Confirmation that the A/C works
- Number and type of travelling seats
- Whether a child seat can be fitted safely
- V5C body type, seating capacity and maximum weight
- Service and MOT history
- Timing-belt or timing-chain information
- Details of any rust, damp, warning lights or known faults
- Paperwork for the pop-top, rear seats and rock-and-roll bed
- Confirmation that the pop-top includes a real sleeping platform

## Reject or strongly flag

- Diesel that is not confirmed Euro 6
- No working air-conditioning
- Only two travelling seats
- No safe position for a child seat
- Serious corrosion or repeated rust advisories
- Damp or leaking pop-top
- Unsafe or undocumented rear seats
- Vehicle weight above 3,500kg
- Major warning lights or unresolved mechanical faults
- Seller unwilling to provide the registration or allow an inspection
- Price above £10,000, or advertised plus VAT where the total exceeds £10,000

## Ideal result

The ideal van would be a 2017–2019 long-wheelbase Expert, Dispatch, Vivaro,
Trafic or Proace with:

- Euro 6 diesel engine
- Working A/C
- Four safe travelling seats
- Child-seat-compatible rear seating
- Pop-top with roof bed
- Rock-and-roll bed
- Approximately 70,000–120,000 miles
- Strong service history
- No serious corrosion or damp
- Purchase price of approximately £7,000–£9,000
